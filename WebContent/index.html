Game of Thrones – Hello There!

Game of Thrones is an American fantasy drama series created by David Benioff and D. B. Weiss. Based on George R. R. Martin’s novel series A Song of Ice and Fire, it explores power struggles, betrayal, and epic battles between the great houses of Westeros.

General Plot

A story of greed, lust, power, and political warfare — seven kingdoms fighting for domination and the Iron Throne.

Warring Kingdoms

  • Kingdom of the North
  • Kingdom of the Mountain and the Vale
  • Kingdom of the Isles and Rivers
  • Kingdom of the Rock
  • Kingdom of the Stormlands
  • Kingdom of the Reach
  • Principality of Dorne
