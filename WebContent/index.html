GAME OF THRONES

Game of Thrones is an American fantasy drama series created by David Benioff & D. B. Weiss. Based on George R. R. Martin’s novel series A Song of Ice and Fire, it explores war, power, betrayal, honor, and the rise and fall of great houses.

General Plot

A ruthless battle for the Iron Throne—filled with greed, lust, loyalty, war, and betrayal.
